### H1: 1. Product Vision and Scope 

 MarketApp will be a digital marketplace and
navigation platform for physical markets. The initial launch will focus on
Lagos, Nigeria, while the architecture will support expansion to other Nigerian
markets without redesigning the core platform. 

 MarketApp will digitally map participating
markets down to individual stalls. Customers will be able to enter a market
virtually, navigate through proprietary 360° imagery, locate shops, browse
products, communicate with shop owners, negotiate prices, pay for orders,
select pickup or delivery, and rate completed transactions. 

 Every
 verified physical shop or stall should have one corresponding digital
 storefront linked to its exact market location.

### H1: 44. Final Product Definition 

 MarketApp
 is a digital platform for discovering, navigating and transacting with
 verified physical market stalls. Customers can virtually walk through
 participating markets using proprietary 360° imagery, find shops and
 products, communicate and negotiate directly with traders, make digital
 purchases, arrange pickup or delivery, and build transaction-based
 reputations for both buyers and sellers. 

 This document is intended as the
consolidated baseline for UX/UI design, technical architecture, database
modeling, API specification, security/compliance planning, development
estimation and phased implementation.

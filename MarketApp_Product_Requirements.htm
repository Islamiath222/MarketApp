MarketApp

Product Requirements Document

Mobile App • Website • Seller Portal • Market Administration • 360° Navigation

A digital platform for discovering, navigating and transacting with verified physical market stalls — beginning in Lagos, Nigeria.

 

Core experience

Market → Virtual Market → Shop → Seller → Negotiation → Order → Payment → Delivery/Pickup → Rating

Prepared as the consolidated baseline specification for product design, system architecture, development planning and vendor estimation.

 

1. Product Vision and Scope

MarketApp will be a digital marketplace and navigation platform for physical markets. The initial launch will focus on Lagos, Nigeria, while the architecture will support expansion to other Nigerian markets without redesigning the core platform.

MarketApp will digitally map participating markets down to individual stalls. Customers will be able to enter a market virtually, navigate through proprietary 360° imagery, locate shops, browse products, communicate with shop owners, negotiate prices, pay for orders, select pickup or delivery, and rate completed transactions.

Every verified physical shop or stall should have one corresponding digital storefront linked to its exact market location.

6. Proprietary 360° Imagery System

MarketApp will own and operate the internal market imagery system. It should not depend on Google Street View for the core in-market experience.

6.1 Field Capture

·  Capture 360° panoramas along major walkways at regular intervals

·  Capture at intersections, entrances, stairs, landmarks and near shops

·  Record panorama ID, market, zone/floor/corridor, X/Y coordinates, heading, capture date, adjacent panoramas and nearby shops

·  Maintain original protected image plus processed/public version

6.2 Viewer Requirements

·  Swipe/drag to look around

·  Gyroscope support

·  Move forward/backward through linked panoramas

·  Turn and follow directional arrows

·  Mini-map and current position

·  Floor switching

·  Clickable shop and amenity hotspots

·  Navigate-to-shop and Enter Shop actions

10. User Accounts and Identity

Both customers and shop owners must create an account. A single user account may hold multiple roles, allowing a merchant to also shop as a customer.

·  Full name

·  Unique verified email address

·  Unique verified phone number

·  Password or passkey

·  Terms and privacy acceptance

·  Account status, creation date and last login

Email uniqueness and phone uniqueness must be enforced at the database level. Emails should be normalized before comparison. Phone numbers should be normalized to E.164 format (for example +2348012345678).

19. Haggling and Structured Offers

Negotiation is a core feature. Customers and sellers may haggle naturally in chat, but the final agreed price must become a structured offer before payment.

Action

Customer / Seller Behavior

Buy at Listed Price

Customer accepts public price

Make an Offer

Customer proposes price and quantity

Counter

Seller or customer proposes revised terms

Accept

Current offer becomes binding checkout terms

Decline

Negotiation ends or returns to chat

 

A structured offer should contain the shop, product snapshot, quantity, original price, agreed unit price, shipping, platform charges if applicable, total, expiration time and status. Offer history must be immutable.

20. Cart, Checkout and Order Creation

For the initial release, each checkout should contain products from only one shop. Multi-shop checkout should be deferred because it complicates negotiation, payment splitting, shipping, refunds and disputes.

·  Create order from accepted structured offer or listed-price purchase

·  Store immutable product snapshot at purchase time

·  Show item totals, negotiated discount, delivery/pickup selection, fees and final total

·  Collect required delivery information only when delivery is selected

21. Payments and Seller Settlement

MarketApp should integrate with appropriately licensed Nigerian payment providers. The platform should avoid launching its own stored-value wallet initially.

·  Support provider-managed seller subaccounts or split payments where available

·  Record successful payment based on secure provider confirmation, not screenshots

·  Separate seller allocation, platform commission and provider charges

·  Maintain payment, refund and settlement history

·  Require strong verification for settlement-account changes

24. Mutual Ratings and Reputation

After a qualifying completed transaction, the customer may rate the shop and the shop may rate the customer. Ratings must be transaction-backed.

·  One review per party per qualifying transaction

·  Customer-to-seller/shop rating is public on the shop profile

·  Seller-to-customer rating is visible to sellers interacting with that customer but not publicly searchable

·  Overall 1–5 star score plus optional written review

·  Public shop reviews marked Verified Transaction

·  Seller may respond publicly to customer review

24.2 Double-Blind Reviews

Recommended: both reviews remain hidden until both sides submit, or until a 7–14 day review window expires. This reduces retaliatory ratings.

25. Review Integrity

·  Cancelled orders normally cannot be reviewed

·  Reviews cannot be transferred between accounts

·  Historical reviews remain linked to the underlying transaction

·  Reviews may be reported for fraud, harassment, spam or personal information

·  Negative reviews are not removed simply because a seller objects

·  Monitor repeated tiny transactions, shared devices, rating clusters and abnormal review velocity

26. Returns, Refunds and Disputes

·  Item not received

·  Wrong or damaged item

·  Item materially differs from listing

·  Counterfeit claim

·  Missing quantity

·  Late/failed delivery

·  Refund disagreement

The dispute record should preserve the original product snapshot, offer history, chat, payment record, shipping/pickup evidence, images, both parties’ submissions, staff notes and final resolution. Relevant evidence must not be silently editable after a dispute begins.

42. Product Differentiator

Physical Market Mapping + Proprietary 360° Navigation + Verified Physical Shops + Shop-Specific Digital Catalogs + Traditional Price Negotiation + Digital Payments + Pickup/Delivery + Transaction-Based Reputation

 

MarketApp should preserve the feel of a real market experience: a customer can enter a Nigerian market from a phone, walk through it virtually, discover a stall, speak directly with the trader, haggle, purchase and receive the item without losing the character of the physical marketplace.

44. Final Product Definition

MarketApp is a digital platform for discovering, navigating and transacting with verified physical market stalls. Customers can virtually walk through participating markets using proprietary 360° imagery, find shops and products, communicate and negotiate directly with traders, make digital purchases, arrange pickup or delivery, and build transaction-based reputations for both buyers and sellers.

 

This document is intended as the consolidated baseline for UX/UI design, technical architecture, database modeling, API specification, security/compliance planning, development estimation and phased implementation.
